Parallel, not composable

  • date 2026-09-20
    Two or more methodologies may answer the same question. Do not blend them. A composite — this one's vocabulary, that one's record, a third one's filter — is a house method that cites nobody, and it inherits none of their authority while claiming all of it. This exact mistake was made and corrected on 2026-09-20: an analysis proposed "adopt A's vocabulary + B's record + C's filter", and the answer was that they are parallel tracks selected by context.
