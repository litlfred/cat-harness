test-report

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/graphTypology/test-report

Test reports -- one `test-report/v1` document per run of a plan against a system under test: per-case verdicts in the block-qa entry shape, a rollup for that ONE plan that may not disagree with its cases, and never a total across plans. `running` is never a pass, and the system under test never writes its own verdict. Generated by the run.

Defined by https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/.

A term of the cat-harness vocabulary (https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/; machine-readable: index.jsonld).
