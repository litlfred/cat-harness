web-annex

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/graphTypology/web-annex

A WHO publication's web annexes, L1 or L2, held as data in the knowledge graph rather than as the files WHO attaches to the publication: a DAK's core data dictionary workbook as JSON (`dak-data-dictionary/v1`, loaded on demand by the IG site's annex view), and the declared mapping from its generic personas to the swimlanes of its business-process figures (`dak-swimlanes/v1`). Every node says which publication it is an annex of (`annexOf`: a library entry when one is held, else the parent's IRIS handle), what the publication calls it (`label`, e.g. "Web Annex A"), its own IRIS handle only when WHO gave it a record (`iris`), and the document-kind section it serves (`section`, e.g. `dak#core-data-elements` or `l1#annexes`).

Defined by https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/.
