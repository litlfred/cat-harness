archimate

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/graphTypology/archimate

ArchiMate models a layer holds, as Archi `.archimate` files (plain XML or Archi's zipped archive), named by `cat-archimate.config.json` (cat-harness's `archimate` subgraph). Every view, element and relationship in a model is a node of its own: a page and an IRI, keyed by Archi's own id, and every view is drawn as SVG from the model's diagram.

Defined by https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/.

A term of the cat-harness vocabulary (https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/; machine-readable: index.jsonld).
