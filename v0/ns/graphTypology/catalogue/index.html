catalogue

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/graphTypology/catalogue

A remote catalogue modelled by reference — communities, collections and items of a corpus the layer does not hold. Every node declares whether its bytes are here (`materialized`), elsewhere (`referenced`) or unestablished (`unknown`), with no default. Distinct from `library`, which is content that IS here.

Defined by https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/.

A term of the cat-harness vocabulary (https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/; machine-readable: index.jsonld).
