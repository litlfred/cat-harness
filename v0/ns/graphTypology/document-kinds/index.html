document-kinds

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/graphTypology/document-kinds

Document kinds — named structures of sections (fixed or semi-fixed) that a document authored with a harness follows, one `document-kind/1.0.0` JSON each, plus computed `document-kind-coverage/1.0.0` reports of how a subject realises one. Every kind and section names its sources; `computedFrom` names the declared graphs a section derives from.

Defined by https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/.

A term of the cat-harness vocabulary (https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/; machine-readable: index.jsonld).
