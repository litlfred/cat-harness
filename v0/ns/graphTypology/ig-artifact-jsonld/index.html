ig-artifact-jsonld

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/graphTypology/ig-artifact-jsonld

One JSON-LD node per artefact of a FHIR Implementation Guide, written from its artefact index beside the artefact's thin page (`artifact/<Type>-<id>.jsonld`): the FHIR RDF class, Dublin Core and schema.org identity, each representation as a `schema:MediaObject`, and PROV for a compiled copy. It is the asset a thin artefact page renders. Beside them, one index node (`artifact/index.jsonld`, `folio-ig-artifact-index-jsonld/v1`) lists every artefact in the Publisher's order, each entry naming its node: what the thin `artifacts` and `toc` pages draw.

Defined by https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/.

A term of the cat-harness vocabulary (https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/; machine-readable: index.jsonld).
