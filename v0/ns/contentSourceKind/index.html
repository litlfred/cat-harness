contentSourceKind

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/contentSourceKind

Which kind of content source: `directory`, `branch`, or a branch `family`, as a plain value. Its own term, NOT `dcterms:type`: that property's range is a class, and the published subgraph files use it as a link (`holdsGraph`), so a literal under it failed publish-verify's object-link check (ld-object-property-is-a-link, 2026-10-03).
