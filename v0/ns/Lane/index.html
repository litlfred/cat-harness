Lane

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/Lane

A swimlane of one BPMN process: part of that process, naming the Role its tasks are performed in (`bindsRole`, BPMN's `partitionElementRef`). Not itself a role — a role is a registry node, and many lanes in many processes name it (#1168, B9b; owner, 2026-10-16).

Defined by https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/.

A term of the cat-harness vocabulary (https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/; machine-readable: index.jsonld).
