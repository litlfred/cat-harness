rationale

https://litlfred.github.io/cat-harness/v0/ns/rationale

Why a convention holds, so a reader can tell whether it still does. Kept separate from the statement deliberately — a rule and its justification collapsed into one field is a rule nobody can retire, because nothing is left that says what would falsify it.
