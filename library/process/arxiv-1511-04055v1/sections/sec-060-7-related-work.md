This section describes related work from several perspectives. First of all, related research in the area of 
visualizing the process of process modeling (PPM) is summarized (see Section 7.1). Next, the usage of 
traditional process modeling notations for the visualization of the PPM is discussed (see Section 7.2). 
Subsequently, a selection of other visualizations that focus on hierarchy and control perspectives is presented 
(see Section 7.3).
