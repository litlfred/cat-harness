- **Study Design:** Empirical between/within-subject comprehension study ($n = 157$ business & IS students, 5 pages) comparing **4 flow directions** (LR, RL, TB, BT) on BPMN models.
- **What Was Measured:** Comprehension accuracy (% correct on local and global verification tasks) and perceived difficulty across the 4 flow directions.
- **Numeric Thresholds & Key Findings:**
  - Confirming preliminary trend: **Right-to-Left (RL)** and **Bottom-to-Top (BT)** flow directions impair comprehension accuracy and increase perceived cognitive load relative to **Left-to-Right (LR)** and **Top-to-Bottom (TB)** for left-to-right script readers (Section 3, pp. 134–135).
