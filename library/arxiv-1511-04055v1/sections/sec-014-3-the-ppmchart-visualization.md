The PPMChart visualization graphically represents data of the process of process modeling (PPM) (see Section 
3.1). The visualization uses timelines on which colored dots are associated with positions that correspond to the 
time when the corresponding PPM operations are recorded for the process model (see Section 3.2). The Dotted 
Chart Analysis plug-in was adapted and extended (see Section 3.3), which resulted in a new plug-in in the ProM 
tool that produces the PPMCharts (see Section 3.4).
