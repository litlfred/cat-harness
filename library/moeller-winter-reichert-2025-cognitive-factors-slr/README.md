## Study Design & Empirical Measurements

- **Method:** Systematic Literature Review (SLR) following Kitchenham & Charters (2007) across **6 digital libraries** (Web of Science: 148, IEEE Xplore: 29, ScienceDirect: 31, ACM Digital Library: 18, PubMed: 42, SpringerLink: 458 $	o$ **726 initial hits** $	o$ 54 after title/duplicates $	o$ 44 after abstract $	o$ 36 after full text $	o$ **$N = 47$ primary studies** after backward/forward snowballing: **41 empirical studies** and **6 theoretical studies**; Section 3.2, Fig. 4 p. 9, Section 3.4 pp. 10–11).
- **Taxonomy & Clusters:**
  - **3 Research Clusters (Fig. 5 & Table 2, p. 11):** Cluster I: *Cognitive Psychology Theories* (**16 studies**, S1–S16), Cluster II: *Cognitive Factors in Process Model Comprehension* (**28 studies**, S17–S20, S22–S26, S28–S46), Cluster III: *Cognitive Neuroscience Techniques* (**3 studies**, S21, S27, S47).
  - **7 Cognitive Categories (Table 1 p. 6, Table 3 pp. 12–13):**
    1. *Perception* (39 of 41 empirical studies: visual perception, pattern recognition, spatial orientation, gestalt principles, color/symbol coding),
    2. *Attention & Concentration* (32 of 41: selective/divided/sustained attention, visual search),
    3. *Memory & Knowledge Representation* (39 of 41: working memory capacity, long-term memory schemas, mental models),
    4. *Language* (15 of 41: syntactic/semantic label processing),
    5. *Problem Solving & Decision Making* (39 of 41: reasoning along control-flow paths and gateways),
    6. *Emotion & Motivation* (12 of 41: cognitive appraisal, physiological arousal, user satisfaction),
    7. *Metacognition & Self-Regulation* (14 of 41: comprehension monitoring, reading strategy adaptation).
- **What Was Measured Across the 41 Empirical Studies:**
  - **Cognitive Neuroscience Measurements (19 of 41 studies = 46.3%, Table 4 p. 15):**
    - *Eye Tracking* (**18 studies**): **Fixations** (count, duration, dwell time on AOIs) in **18/18 (100%)**, **Scan paths** in **9/18 (50%)**, **Saccades** in **5/18 (27.8%)**; no study measured pupil dilation or blink rate (Table 4, p. 15).
    - *Electrodermal Activity (EDA)* (**1 study**, S30): Skin Conductance Level (SCL) and Skin Conductance Response (SCR) peaks/amplitudes (pp. 15–16).
  - **Cognitive Psychology Theories Applied (37 of 41 studies = 90.2%, Table 5 p. 16):**
    - *Cognitive Load Theory (CLT)* in **37/37 (100%)** (intrinsic, extraneous, and germane cognitive load),
    - *Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning (CTML)* in **6 studies**,
    - *Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)* in **6 studies**,
    - *Cognitive Fit Theory (CFT)* in **5 studies**,
    - *Dual Coding Theory (DCT)* in **2 studies**.
- **Numeric Thresholds Stated:** Synthesizes cognitive load and visual perception mechanisms rather than defining new numeric diagram-size cutoffs; cites Miller's working memory capacity limit and Moody's (2009) Physics of Notations principles across the reviewed primary studies (pp. 4–5, 16–20).
